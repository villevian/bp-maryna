6Complete the sentences with these words from the video.Do it online
flightgatelocationloungepublic transportationreservationridesharing appstayvehicle
  1. 1They made a for two rooms, but when they arrived, only one was available.
    make a reservation = book something in advance. “for two rooms” tells us it’s a hotel booking. Not “stay” or “flight” — you don’t make a flight for two rooms.
    make a reservation = забронювати. Слова «for two rooms» підказують, що це бронювання в готелі. Не «stay» чи «flight»: рейс не бронюють «на дві кімнати».
  2. 2I took a thirteen-hour from Madrid to Buenos Aires.
    “thirteen-hour … from Madrid to Buenos Aires” = a long journey between two countries by plane → flight. We say take a flight.
    «Тринадцятигодинний … з Мадрида до Буенос-Айреса» — це довга подорож літаком між країнами → flight (рейс). Кажемо take a flight.
  3. 3We need to hire a to take ten people from the airport to the conference centre.
    vehicle = a car, van or bus. You hire a vehicle to take ten people. Not “ridesharing app” — you use an app, you don’t hire it.
    vehicle = транспортний засіб (авто, мікроавтобус). Для десяти людей його hire, тобто орендують. Не «ridesharing app»: застосунок не орендують, ним користуються.
  4. 4He usually uses a to get a car from one meeting to the next.
    He uses something to get a car → an app where you order a car and a driver: ridesharing app. Not “vehicle” — “use a vehicle to get a car” doesn’t make sense.
    Він uses щось, щоб get a car, тобто викликати машину → ridesharing app (Uber, Bolt). Не «vehicle»: «використати транспортний засіб, щоб отримати машину» не має сенсу.
  5. 5I arrived at the five minutes after the plane had left.
    “after the plane had left” → the place where you get on the plane: gate. Not “lounge” — you wait in the lounge, but the plane leaves from the gate. We say at the gate.
    «Після того як літак вилетів» → місце посадки в літак: gate (вихід на посадку). Не «lounge»: у лаунжі чекають, а сідають у літак біля gate. Кажемо at the gate.
  6. 6We had a comfortable in Vietnam because the resort was very modern.
    a comfortable stay = the time you spend in a hotel or resort. “because the resort was very modern” describes the hotel experience.
    a comfortable stay = комфортне перебування в готелі чи курорті. «Бо курорт був дуже сучасний» описує саме перебування.
  7. 7They waited in the departure until it was time to get on the plane.
    departure lounge is a fixed phrase: the room at the airport where you wait for your flight. Not “gate” — you don’t “wait in the departure gate” until it’s time to board.
    departure lounge — стала фраза: зал очікування в аеропорту. Не «gate»: до посадки чекають у залі, а не «в гейті».
  8. 8The of the apartment is perfect – near the office and the station.
    “near the office and the station” describes where the apartment is → location.
    «Поруч з офісом і вокзалом» описує, де розташована квартира → location (розташування).
  9. 9I like to take so I can see what life in the city is like.
    take public transportation = buses, trains, metro. You travel with local people, so you see real city life. Public transportation is uncountable: no “-s”.
    take public transportation = їздити громадським транспортом. Їдеш разом з місцевими, тому бачиш справжнє життя міста. Слово незлічуване, без «-s».
7AMatch the words in bold with the definitions.Do it online
  1. 1I go abroad for work trips quite often.  
    abroad = to or in another country (c). We say go abroad, not go to abroad.
    abroad = за кордон / за кордоном (c). Кажемо go abroad, без «to».
  2. 2I take several short-haul flights each year.  
    short-haul = a flight that travels a short distance (a). “Haul” = distance; the opposite is long-haul.
    short-haul = рейс на коротку відстань (a). «Haul» = відстань; протилежне — long-haul.
  3. 3The flight was delayed by two hours.  
    delayed = late, usually because of a problem (e). delayed by two hours — use “by” for how long.
    delayed = затриманий, зазвичай через проблему (e). delayed by two hours — тривалість затримки з «by».
  4. 4The captain said that we were ready for landing.  
    landing = bringing a plane down to the ground (b). The captain says “ready for landing” just before the plane comes down.
    landing = посадка літака (b). Капітан каже «ready for landing» перед приземленням.
  5. 5The departure was a few minutes late.  
    departure = the action of leaving, especially at the start of a journey (d). Look for “leaving” in the definition.
    departure = відправлення, виліт (d). Ключове слово у визначенні — «leaving».
  1. aa flight or plane that travels a short distance
  2. bthe action of bringing a plane down to the ground after being in the air
  3. cto or in another country
  4. dthe action of leaving a place, especially at the start of a journey
  5. elate, usually because of a problem
BWrite the words in bold in Exercise 7A with their opposites.Extra activity
  1. 1long-haul  
    long-haul ↔ short-haul: long distance vs short distance.
    long-haul ↔ short-haul: далекий рейс ↔ близький.
  2. 2take-off  
    take-off ↔ landing: the plane goes up vs comes down.
    take-off ↔ landing: зліт ↔ посадка.
  3. 3arrival  
    arrival ↔ departure: you come vs you leave. At airports: Arrivals / Departures.
    arrival ↔ departure: прибуття ↔ відправлення. В аеропорту табло Arrivals / Departures.
  4. 4on-time  
    on-time ↔ delayed: at the planned time vs late. (“late” is also OK.)
    on-time ↔ delayed: вчасно ↔ із затримкою. («late» теж зараховуємо.)
  5. 5local  
    local ↔ abroad: in your own area vs in another country. (“international / foreign” are also OK.)
    local ↔ abroad: місцевий ↔ закордонний. («international / foreign» теж зараховуємо.)
