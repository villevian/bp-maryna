🔊 1.01

Ira: Hi, Dan. Have you got a minute?

Dan: Oh, hi, Ira. Sure. How are things?

Ira: Fine, thanks. But we need to change the date of the sprint planning meeting, sorry. Are you available on Friday the 29th, in the morning?

Dan: Sorry, no, I’m not. I have a demo for a client on Friday morning, until eleven. Let me check my calendar… Then I have a call with the Berlin team at twelve, for an hour. I’m available in the afternoon. How about Friday afternoon?

Ira: Sorry, I’m afraid I’m busy then. I meet clients on Friday afternoon from two o’clock. Wait… How about lunchtime on Friday?

Dan: Friday lunchtime is good. I’m available. What time, exactly?

Ira: I usually have lunch at one o’clock. How about then?

Dan: Sounds good. Shall we meet in the meeting room Kyiv?

Ira: Yes, that’s fine. Then we can go for lunch after the meeting.

Dan: OK, so the new sprint planning meeting is at one o’clock on Friday the 29th. I’ll update the invite in Google Calendar. See you then.

Ira: Thanks. See you then!
